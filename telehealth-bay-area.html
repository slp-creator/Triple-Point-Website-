Why Bay Area families choose telehealth with Lauren

Specialized care, close to home.

Providers who hold both an ASHA speech-language pathology certification and advanced orofacial myofunctional therapy training (IAOM, The Breathe Institute) are rare — including within the Bay Area itself. Telehealth removes the distance barrier, so families in San Francisco, the East Bay, the Peninsula, and the South Bay can access this specialized combination of training without it depending on who happens to have an office nearby.

Lauren works with mouth breathing, dysfunctional swallow patterns, TMJ dysfunction, bruxism, orthodontic relapse, sleep-disordered breathing, tongue and lip tie follow-up care, articulation and speech clarity, and early communication delays — treating the underlying cause rather than just the symptom.

Being upfront about what's fully remote and what isn't: Early Intervention Parent/Caregiver Coaching (ages 0–4) is available 100% via telehealth — no travel required. Orofacial myofunctional therapy typically requires 1–2 in-person visits at the Truckee clinic for the evaluation and early treatment sessions, with ongoing sessions continuing via telehealth afterward. Many Bay Area families combine those visits with a weekend trip to Tahoe. Please inquire directly about your specific situation.

This service is a good fit if you're located in:
