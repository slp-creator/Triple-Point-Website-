Do any of these sound familiar?

Orofacial myofunctional disorders are more common than most people realize.

The symptoms below can appear in isolation or together. If several resonate with you or your child, a myofunctional evaluation may help identify the underlying cause.

Sleep & Breathing

  • Mouth breathing or open-mouth posture
  • Snoring or sleep apnea
  • Restless sleep or feeling unrested
  • Chronic nasal congestion
  • In children: bedwetting, night terrors

Dental & Jaw

  • Tongue tie or lip tie
  • Crowded teeth or malocclusion
  • Orthodontic relapse after braces
  • TMJ pain or jaw tension
  • Bruxism (clenching or grinding)

Eating & Swallowing

  • Difficulty swallowing food or pills
  • Texture aversions or picky eating
  • Chronic acid reflux
  • In infants: poor latch, slow weight gain
  • Slow or messy eating

Speech & Oral Habits

  • Articulation errors or unclear speech
  • Thumb sucking, nail biting
  • Prolonged pacifier or bottle use
  • Excessive drooling
  • Difficulty with solid foods

Head, Neck & Pain

  • Frequent headaches or migraines
  • Chronic neck and shoulder tension
  • Recurring ENT infections
  • Hypersensitive gag reflex
  • Facial asymmetry

Not sure if this applies to you?

Many of these symptoms have multiple causes. A free 30-minute consultation is the right place to start. Lauren will help you determine whether a myofunctional evaluation makes sense for your situation.
