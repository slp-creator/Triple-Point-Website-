About Lauren

Over a decade of clinical experience — and a personal reason to specialize.

Triple Point Speech Therapy's goal is to bring greater awareness of myofunctional disorders to local and regional communities, empower patients and families, and improve their quality of life. My personal history with tongue tie, sleep apnea, TMJ dysfunction, and bruxism — combined with clinical treatment and training from leaders in the field — has given me a nuanced understanding of myofunctional disorders. My goal is to guide patients with efficient, effective, and caring treatment — to bring them to a place of ease and balance with breathing, eating, and speaking.
