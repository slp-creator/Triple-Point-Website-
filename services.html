Orofacial Myofunctional Therapy

Restoring functional breathing, chewing, swallowing, and speech patterns.

Orofacial myofunctional therapy (OMT) uses structured, evidence-based exercises to correct dysfunction in the muscles that control breathing, swallowing, and speech. It addresses the underlying patterns that drive a wide range of symptoms — rather than treating each symptom in isolation.

OMT is appropriate for children and adults, and is often recommended by dentists, orthodontists, and ENTs as part of an integrated treatment plan.

  • Mouth breathing and open-mouth resting posture
  • Dysfunctional swallow patterns
  • Tongue tie (before and after lip and tongue tie release)
  • Sleep-disordered breathing and snoring
  • Orthodontic relapse prevention
  • TMJ dysfunction and jaw tension

Tongue Tie Support

Before and after lip and tongue tie release.

Tongue tie (ankyloglossia) and lip tie can affect feeding, speech, breathing, and sleep at every age — from newborns struggling to latch to adults with unexplained jaw tension. Lauren provides comprehensive evaluation and pre- and post-release myofunctional therapy to support optimal outcomes from release procedures.

  • Infant feeding and latch difficulties
  • Pre-release myofunctional preparation
  • Post-release rehabilitation and scar management exercises
  • Coordination with tongue tie–informed dentists and ENTs
